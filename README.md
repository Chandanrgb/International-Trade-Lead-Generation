---
🚀 Executive Summary
This repository showcases comprehensive international market research, B2B data mining, and trade lead generation executed for Westar by India. The project involved end-to-end data acquisition, verification, and enrichment of commercial importers, food distributors, and global trading partners across high-growth international corridors (GCC, Gulf Food sectors, and South Africa).
The resulting structured datasets served as core intelligence assets for outbound business development, regional market penetration, and strategic sales targeting.
---
📊 Project Scope & Dataset Architecture
The repository is segmented into three major regional intelligence modules:
Module / Directory	Target Market / Scope	Records Mapped	Key Data Attributes Captured
`01_GCC_Data/`	Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Region	1,658 Records	Company Name, Country, Import Transaction History, Trade Reasons, Business Verticals, Verified Email, Phone, LinkedIn, Official Website
`02_Gulf_Food_Data/`	Gulf Food Sector Exhibitors & Buyers	77 Records	Company Name, Country, Product Categories, Key Contact Persons, Direct Phone Numbers, Corporate Emails, Websites
`03_South_Africa_Data/`	South African Trade & Import Market	778 Record Sets	Company Name, Transaction Volumes, Active Trade Values, Business Lines, Trading Commodities, Verified Contact Emails, LinkedIn Profiles
---
🛠️ Methodology & Data Engineering Workflow
To transform raw, unstructured business names into high-intent, actionable sales pipeline assets, a rigorous manual and programmatic data discovery workflow was executed:
Entity Identification & Source Extraction:
Extracted raw company identities and baseline trade signals directly from enterprise directories, export-import databases, and trade show logs.
Deep-Dive Digital Footprint Tracing:
Conducted granular multi-channel searches for each business entity to trace official web properties, corporate domains, and active digital storefronts.
Executive Contact & Communication Mapping:
Uncovered key decision-makers, verified corporate email architectures, direct telephone lines, and active professional networking profiles (LinkedIn).
Data Cleansing, Normalization & Structuring:
Validated data integrity, normalized text fields across disparate naming conventions, and compiled clean, production-ready master Excel worksheets.
